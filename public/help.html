What RepostRx does

You write an article once. RepostRx helps you share it:

  1. It reads your article from its link (or from text you paste).
  2. It writes draft posts for LinkedIn, X and Facebook, in your voice, with a link back to the article.
  3. You edit and schedule the drafts for the day and time you choose.
  4. It keeps track of your posts, and explains in plain English if one fails.

The posting itself is done by Blotato, a scheduling service connected to your social accounts. You'll see its name in a few places; my.blotato.com is where your accounts and scheduled posts live.

Signing in

RepostRx is protected by one password, so strangers can't use it to post to your accounts.

  • Type the password at the top of the page and click Sign in.
  • RepostRx remembers you on this browser, so you won't need to sign in again on the same computer.
  • On a shared or borrowed computer, click Sign out when you're done. That makes the browser forget the password.
  • Never send the password by email or text. If you think someone else has it, ask whoever set up RepostRx to change it.

Demo mode

If you were given a demo password, you can try RepostRx without being able to post anything. You'll see a banner saying "Demo mode: scheduling is turned off."

  • You can: import an article or paste text, read and edit the drafts, add sample cards to the board, use Post Doctor, and read this help page.
  • You can't: schedule posts, see the connected social accounts, or check real posts. RepostRx's server refuses these for the demo password, so nothing you do in demo mode can post.

Importing an article

Under 1. Import an article, paste the article's web address (it starts with https://) and click Import. RepostRx shows its progress, then writes your drafts automatically.

If the import doesn't work: paste the text instead

Some websites, including Substack and LinkedIn articles, don't let RepostRx read the article itself; it only sees the page's menus, buttons or a sign-in page. When that happens, RepostRx tells you so instead of writing drafts about nothing.

  1. Open your article, select all of its text, and copy it.
  2. Paste it into Or paste the article text.
  3. Paste the article's address into Article link (optional, but recommended: it's added to each draft so readers can find the full article).
  4. Click Write drafts from pasted text.

Your drafts

Under 2. Edit your drafts you'll find three drafts. They're written by AI, so always read them before scheduling: check names, numbers and anything that sounds unlike you.

LinkedIn
A few short paragraphs, ending with a question or takeaway.
X
One short post. X allows 280 characters; the counter under the box turns red if you go over. Links count as 23 characters, however long they really are.
Facebook
A friendly draft you can copy and post yourself. RepostRx can't schedule to Facebook yet.

If you imported from a link, the link is already at the end of each draft.

Compliance footer

  • Your disclosures (name, NMLS numbers, Equal Opportunity wording) are added after the link on every new draft, from the Compliance footer boxes at the top of the page.
  • X uses the short footer, and it counts toward the 280 characters, so the X draft is written shorter to leave room.
  • You can edit the boxes before importing. Edits last until you reload the page; whoever set up RepostRx can change the saved footer.
  • When you schedule, RepostRx warns you if the footer is missing from the post or was changed. Read the warning before you click Confirm and schedule.
  • In demo mode the boxes start empty, so you can type your own.

Scheduling a post

Nothing is posted until you confirm. RepostRx always shows you a summary first, and only schedules the post after you click Confirm and schedule.
  1. Under each draft, check Posts to: the account the post will go to.
  2. Choose When to post. Times are in your computer's time zone, and must be at least 5 minutes from now.
  3. Click Schedule LinkedIn post… (or Schedule X post…).
  4. Read the summary: account, time and the exact text.
  5. Click Confirm and schedule once, or Cancel to go back.

You'll see Scheduled ✓ with the time, and the post is added to Your posts.

After scheduling: editing the draft in RepostRx does not change the scheduled post. To change the text or time, or to cancel, go to my.blotato.com. Scheduling the same draft twice creates two posts.

The "Your posts" board

Every post you schedule appears here, with its current status from Blotato. RepostRx checks the statuses when you open the page; click Refresh statuses to check again.

Scheduled
Waiting for its time. Nothing to do.
Published
It's live. Click View live post ↗ to see it.
In progress
Blotato is posting it right now. This usually takes a minute or two.
⚠ Check
Still "in progress" after 10 minutes. Look for the post at my.blotato.com, or click Diagnose.
Failed
The post didn't go out. Click Diagnose to find out why.

Good to know

  • The board is saved in this browser only. On another computer or browser it starts empty, but your posts are still scheduled.
  • Remove from board only removes the card from RepostRx. It does not delete or cancel the post.
  • To add a post that isn't on the board, paste the post's ID into Add an existing post. That's the long code RepostRx showed when you scheduled it (labelled "postSubmissionId"), made of letters, numbers and dashes.
  • The Add a sample… buttons add practice cards marked SAMPLE. They aren't real posts; remove them when you're done.

Post Doctor

Post Doctor explains why a post failed, in plain English, and suggests what to do. It only explains; it never posts, retries or deletes anything.

Using it

  • From the board: click Diagnose on a Failed or ⚠ Check card. Post Doctor fills itself in and shows the answer.
  • From an error message: paste the error into Error, add the status code if you have it (a number like 401 or 422), and click Diagnose.
  • No error, just a problem? Choose a Symptom, such as "Post went out twice".

Reading the answer

Known cause
A problem RepostRx has seen before, with a tested explanation and fix.
Needs details
There are two possible causes. Paste the full error (including its "details" part) so Post Doctor can tell which.
AI suggestion — review before sending
A new problem. AI wrote this answer from the error alone, so read every sentence and check any warnings before using it.

Each answer has a customer reply you can edit and copy, and an internal note for whoever handles support.

Don't paste passwords or keys into Post Doctor. If one slips in, Post Doctor hides it before doing anything else.

The daily email report

If your RepostRx is set up with the daily report, you get an email every morning at 8 AM (Pacific time), even on quiet days, so you know the check ran.

"RepostRx daily report: 0 failed, 0 overdue"
All good. The email lists what was published in the last 24 hours and what's coming up this week.
"⚠ RepostRx daily report: 1 failed, 0 overdue"
Something needs a look. The email shows each problem post's time, account and error. Paste the error into Post Doctor.
Overdue
A post that should have gone out more than 10 minutes ago but is still waiting. Check it at my.blotato.com.
"⚠ RepostRx daily report couldn't run"
The check itself didn't work, so this is not an all-clear. Tell whoever set up RepostRx.

The email numbers posts the way Blotato does (for example "Blotato post 1234567"). That's a different code from the one on your board, so match posts by their date and time.

Common problems

"Wrong or missing RepostRx password"
Check you're using the current password, with no extra spaces. If you have more than one saved password for RepostRx, delete the old one so your browser can't fill in the wrong one.
Something is missing, or looks different from this guide
Your browser may be showing an old copy of the page. Press F5 (or Ctrl + R; on a Mac, ⌘ + R) to reload it. The version line at the bottom of the RepostRx page shows which version you're on.
"The imported page didn't include the article itself"
The website (often Substack or LinkedIn) only showed RepostRx its menus or a sign-in page. Use paste the text instead.
The page says "Demo mode"
You signed in with the demo password, so scheduling is turned off. That's expected. See Demo mode. To schedule, sign out and sign in with the main password.
The X counter is red
The X draft is over 280 characters, so RepostRx won't schedule it. Shorten it until the counter is back to normal. Remember that the link counts as 23 characters.

Still stuck? Copy the exact message you see and send it, with the time it happened, to whoever set up RepostRx.
